About Me

Welcome,

I am a software engineer with a passion for developing innovative and performative end-to-end solutions for complex problems. My fulfillment comes in the form of learning new things, and I take pride in engineering accessible, efficient, and scalable applications.

I am currently using my decade of solo & on the job experience to build the future of energy as a Senior Fullstack Engineer at CLEAResult. I am a tech lead and contributer to the enrollment application engineering project, using best practices to design and develop a seamless customer experience. I am also one of CLEAResult's core engineers in the artificial intelligence development initiative, and have engineered scalable backend solutions that leverage both cloud and AI systems.

My previous work was at IHeartMedia, developing software across a number of different platforms. I kicked off my professional career as the company's first ever Devops Engineering Intern, building deployment pipelines and serverless monitoring applications for the platform team. Then, after completing my Computer Science / Mathematics degree, I was recruited into IHeart's software engineering space, where I worked with our team to design and develop a variety of full stack applications for ad tech and scheduling optimization.

When I am not working on the next big thing, you can find me doing any of my other hobbies! I practice martial arts, love to play indie video games, and have a passion for travel and linguistics.
